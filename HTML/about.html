Our Story

Bean & Bite Bakery was established in 2021 by two childhood friends and passionate culinary enthusiasts, Maya and Liam. What began as a small weekend stall at local farmers' markets quickly became popular, thanks to Liam’s signature 48-hour fermented sourdough and Maya’s creative seasonal pastry flavours.

As more people in the community supported and requested their products, they opened their first physical storefront in 2023. They transformed a historic brick building into a warm and inviting neighbourhood bakery that welcomes both remote workers and local families.

Today, Bean & Bite Bakery continues to combine traditional artisan baking techniques with a modern, comfortable space, creating a unique experience for everyone who visits.

Founder's Journey

Maya and Liam are childhood friends who share a passion for culinary creativity, artisan baking and bringing people together through food and coffee.

Their journey began with a small weekend stall at local farmers' markets. Liam's signature 48-hour fermented sourdough and Maya's creative seasonal pastry flavours quickly gained popularity among members of the local community.

As demand for their products increased, the support from the community encouraged Maya and Liam to take their business to the next level. In 2023, they opened their first physical storefront.

They transformed a historic brick building into a warm and inviting neighbourhood bakery that welcomes both remote workers and local families.

Today, their journey continues as Bean & Bite Bakery combines traditional artisan baking techniques with a modern, comfortable space and a strong focus on community.

Ingredient Sourcing

Bean & Bite Bakery is committed to serving handcrafted, slow-fermented baked goods and exceptional artisan coffee made with fresh, locally sourced ingredients.

The bakery values its relationships with local ingredient suppliers and highlights these relationships as part of its commitment to the local community.

By using locally sourced ingredients, Bean & Bite Bakery aims to maintain the quality of its products while supporting local suppliers and contributing to a stronger community.

The bakery also places importance on sustainable practices and environmentally responsible choices as part of its long-term approach to artisan baking.

Our Mission

At Bean & Bite Bakery, our mission is to bring people together by serving exceptional artisan coffee and handcrafted, slow-fermented baked goods made with fresh, locally sourced ingredients. We are committed to creating a warm and welcoming environment where every customer feels valued and at home, one cup of coffee and one bite at a time.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the leading example of sustainable artisan baking and community-focused café culture in the region. We aim to be recognised for our creativity, commitment to environmentally responsible practices, and our ability to create meaningful connections within the community through great food and genuine hospitality.
